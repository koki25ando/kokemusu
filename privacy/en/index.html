Kokemusu Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Kokemusu (the "App") helps you keep your distance from the apps and websites you tend to open without thinking. It is available on Android and iOS, and this policy applies to both. The developer of the App (the "Developer") cares about your privacy and handles only the minimum information needed, and only on your device.

1. Information the App handles

InformationPurposeWhere it is stored
The apps, websites and keywords you choose to watchTo show the pause screen when you open themOn your device only
Time spent today on watched apps and sites, and your daily limitsTo check whether a limit has been reached and show it on screenOn your device only
Your drops, jar stage, sunny streak, moss guide and similar recordsTo show your progress in the appOn your device only

This information is never sent to the Developer's servers or to any third party. The App only communicates with Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS) to make or check purchases of the paid plan, and none of the information above is sent at that time.

2. Use of the Accessibility service (Android)

On Android, the App uses the Android Accessibility service. Only if you turn it on in your settings, it is used solely for the following:

  • To detect which app is opened in the foreground, and show the pause screen if it is an app you chose
  • Only while a browser (such as Chrome) is in the foreground, to read the URL shown in the address bar and check whether it matches a website or keyword you chose

URLs that are read are used only for this check, and are never saved or sent. The App does not read any other screen content, text you type, passwords, messages or the like.

You can turn off the Accessibility service at any time under Settings > Accessibility on your device.

3. Use of Screen Time (iOS)

On iOS, the App uses Apple's Screen Time APIs (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). Only if you allow it, they are used solely for the following:

  • To show the pause screen (shield) on the apps, categories and websites you selected in Apple's picker once their daily limit is used up
  • To receive notice that usage of the selected apps has reached a threshold such as your limit, and use it for your drops and weather records

Selected apps and websites are handled on your device only, as identifiers issued by Apple. The App does not receive app names or the content of pages you visit. Usage data is not used for any other purpose, and is never used for advertising or shared with third parties. You can revoke Screen Time access at any time under Settings > Screen Time on your device.

4. Paid plan (Kokemusu+)

Purchases, renewals and cancellations of the paid plan are handled through Google Play on Android and the App Store on iOS. Your payment information is managed by Google or Apple, and the Developer never receives it. The only thing the Developer can know is whether your purchase is active. For how payment information is handled, see Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Ads and analytics

The App does not show ads. It also does not include any analytics or tracking tools that send usage data outside your device.

6. Deleting your information

Everything the App stores is on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes all of it. On Android, you can also delete just the data under Settings > Apps > Kokemusu > Storage.

7. Children

The App is not directed primarily at children under 13. The App does not collect personally identifiable information from any user, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page along with the date of the last update. If we make significant changes to how information is handled, we will also let you know in the App.
